Fishy

How to play

  • Eat fish smaller than you to grow. Bigger meals are worth more points.
  • Fish with a red outline are bigger than you. Touch one and you get eaten.
  • Sharks can't be eaten, and some suddenly double back… until you're over halfway to winning. Then nothing can hurt you, and everything is food!
  • Watch out for fishing hooks! Bite one and you get reeled up out of the water… unless you're big enough to bite right through the line.
  • Grow until you nearly fill the screen to win.
