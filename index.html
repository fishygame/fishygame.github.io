    How to play

    • Eat fish smaller than you to grow.
    • Classic: the closer a fish is to your size, the more points it's worth, so go for the big ones!
    • Speed: every fish is worth the same, but eat them one right after another to build a combo, up to x10. Win fast for a time bonus too.
    • Fish with a red outline are bigger than you. Touch one and you get eaten. On Hard there are no outlines, so you'll have to judge for yourself!
    • Sharks can't be eaten, and the ones with red eyes suddenly double back… until you're over halfway to winning. Then nothing can hurt you, and everything is food!
    • Watch out for fishing hooks! Bite one and you get reeled up out of the water… unless you're big enough to bite right through the line.
    • And keep away from the crab on the sand. Its pinch is deadly… unless you're big enough to eat it.
    • Grow until you nearly fill the screen to win.
    • Easy has no sharks or crab, and more to eat at the start. Hard has more sharks (and they all double back), more hooks and more crabs.
