Eat smaller fish, steer clear of bigger ones, and grow until you're the biggest fish in the sea... big enough to fill the whole screen!

**How to play**

- Eat fish smaller than you to grow. Bigger meals are worth more points.
- Fish with a red outline are bigger than you. Touch one and you get eaten.
- Sharks can't be eaten, and the red-eyed ones suddenly double back!
- Watch out for fishing hooks. Bite one and you get reeled right out of the water.
- Keep clear of the crab scuttling along the sand. Its pinch is deadly.
- Make it over halfway and the tables turn: nothing can hurt you, and it's a feeding frenzy.

**Controls**

- Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to swim, Space to pause, M to mute.
- Touch: drag anywhere to swim. Buttons in the corner pause and mute.

Your five best scores are saved in your browser with your initials, so you're always chasing your own record.
