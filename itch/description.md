Eat smaller fish, steer clear of bigger ones, and grow until you're the biggest fish in the sea... big enough to fill the whole screen!

**How to play**

- Eat fish smaller than you to grow.
- Classic mode: the closer a fish is to your size, the more points it's worth.
- Speed mode: chain bites one after another for a combo up to x10, and win fast for a time bonus.
- Fish with a red outline are bigger than you. Touch one and you get eaten.
- Sharks can't be eaten, and the red-eyed ones suddenly double back!
- Watch out for fishing hooks. Bite one and you get reeled right out of the water.
- Keep clear of the crab scuttling along the sand. Its pinch is deadly.
- Make it over halfway and the tables turn: nothing can hurt you, and it's a feeding frenzy.
- Easy: no sharks or crab. Hard: more of everything, and no red outlines to warn you.

**Controls**

- Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to swim, Space to pause, N for the next song, M to mute.
- Touch: drag anywhere to swim. Buttons in the corner pause, skip the song and mute.
- Pick your songs, shuffled or in order, and gulp or burp, on the Sound screen.

Your five best scores for each mode and difficulty are saved in your browser with your initials, so you're always chasing your own record.
